# Fast Pass: From Rough Notes to a Usable Answer

Use this mode when the candidate supplies fragments, a transcript, mock feedback, or an answer needed soon.

## Step 1: Diagnose in five lines

Return:

- **Prompt signal:** the exact competency being tested.
- **Current proof:** what the facts already demonstrate.
- **Mismatch:** the strongest adjacent competency that could distract the answer.
- **Verdict-changing gap:** the one missing fact most likely to raise or lower the assessment.
- **Thesis:** what the interviewer should conclude in one sentence.

Do not give a generic list of every Staff+ trait.

## Step 2: Make the edit visible

### Cut

Remove chronology, repeated setup, secondary projects, unsupported adjectives, and technical detail that does not support the prompt.

### Keep

Retain the candidate-owned decision, credible constraint, strongest mechanism, changed stakeholder behavior, and defensible result.

### Add

Request only missing evidence that changes question fit, agency, judgment, consequence, or credibility.

## Step 3: Draft now

Write the strongest current CARL even if some details are missing.

- Use `[fill: exact real fact]` where a stronger claim depends on evidence.
- Use narrower language when a fact cannot be recovered.
- Never replace a missing result with an expected benefit.
- Prefer three numbered actions and two numbered results when the candidate needs a memorable speaking spine.
- Put the end-user or customer consequence before the organizational consequence.

## Step 4: Ask a high-yield batch

Ask no more than five numbered questions. Each should unlock one of:

1. a candidate-owned decision;
2. the strongest alternative and why it lost;
3. a concrete technical or organizational mechanism;
4. causal or measured consequence;
5. durable behavior, capability, or prevention.

Do not ask questions whose answers belong only in an optional deep dive.

## Step 5: Build interview control

Create six follow-ups with 30-60 second answers. Cover ownership, tradeoff, depth, quality, causality, and limitation. Answers should begin with the conclusion, not repeat the project context.

## Quality bar

The user should be able to rehearse the result immediately and know exactly which facts are worth recovering next.

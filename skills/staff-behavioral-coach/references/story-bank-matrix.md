## Coverage audit

- [ ] Strategic leadership and prioritization
- [ ] Complex technical investigation
- [ ] Architecture under constraints
- [ ] Multi-team influence without authority
- [ ] Conflict or trust repair
- [ ] Ambiguity and incomplete information
- [ ] Significant failure or difficult feedback
- [ ] Developing an individual
- [ ] Building organizational capability
- [ ] Innovation and iteration
- [ ] Data changing judgment
- [ ] Quality, privacy, reliability, or safety
- [ ] AI-driven impact and continuous AI learning, when genuinely supported

Do not force every box to use a different project. Reuse a project only when a different decision and signal are genuinely central.

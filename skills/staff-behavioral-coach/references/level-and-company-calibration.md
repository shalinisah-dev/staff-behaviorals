# Level and Company Calibration

## Staff+ evidence

| Signal | What strong evidence looks like |
|---|---|
| Scope | Consequence beyond the candidate's immediate task or team |
| Ambiguity | Defined the important problem and operating path |
| Influence | Changed decisions or behavior outside direct authority |
| Judgment | Made consequential tradeoffs under constraints |
| Technical depth | Can explain mechanisms, failure modes, and safety boundaries |
| Quality | Reliability, privacy, correctness, maintainability, and rollout are first-class outcomes |
| Multiplier | Created capability, standards, or ownership that persisted |
| Ownership | Personal contribution is distinguishable from team execution |

## Level is agency, not size

- **Senior:** leads a difficult project and resolves local ambiguity.
- **Staff:** shapes team or multi-team goals, architecture, roadmap, staffing, delegation, or operating mechanisms.
- **Senior Staff+:** changes durable direction or capability across an organization through mechanisms that work without constant personal intervention.

These are working heuristics, not universal title mappings.

## Company calibration

Interview loops change. Use this order:

1. recruiter-confirmed competencies and format;
2. exact interviewer wording;
3. official current company guidance;
4. the general Staff+ signals above.

Large companies often emphasize structured evidence, cross-team influence, and calibrated level. Startups may put more weight on speed, range, direct business consequence, constrained resources, and willingness to operate across boundaries. Neither removes the need for technical judgment, quality, or honest attribution.

When mapping to a named company:

- cite the source and date if browsing is available;
- distinguish official guidance from interview-coaching heuristics;
- do not claim a stable universal rubric;
- preserve the same factual story while changing emphasis, not facts.

## Headline

In one or two sentences, state the problem, your strategic or technical contribution, and the outcome.

## CARL

### Context

What mattered to users or the organization? Why was it hard?

### Actions

1. **Decision:** What did you personally decide, and why?
2. **Influence or technical mechanism:** What changed because of your work?
3. **Risk and quality:** What could have failed, and how did you protect it?

### Results

1. **End user or customer:**
2. **Business or organization:**
3. **What persisted:**

### Learning

What mechanism, standard, or later behavior changed?

## Follow-up bank

1. What was your specific contribution?
2. What was the strongest alternative?
3. What made the problem technically or organizationally difficult?
4. What could have gone wrong?
5. How did you validate the result?
6. What did another team or leader change because of your influence?
7. What would not have happened without you?
8. What remained imperfect?

## Rehearsal checks

- [ ] Headline lands within 15 seconds.
- [ ] First pass is under two minutes.
- [ ] Context is no more than two sentences.
- [ ] "I" and "we" accurately separate ownership.
- [ ] At least one alternative or tradeoff is visible.
- [ ] Quality is explicit, not implied.
- [ ] Results are labeled as shipped, measured, inferred, or expected.
- [ ] Learning is demonstrated by a later mechanism or behavior.

# Adversarial Review Rubric

## Staff+ dimensions

Score each as strong, mixed, weak, or not relevant:

- exact question fit;
- problem selection and ambiguity;
- candidate-owned judgment;
- influence beyond authority;
- technical mechanism and quality boundary;
- causal and measurable consequence;
- durable organizational leverage;
- concise, credible communication.

One story does not need every dimension. It must strongly prove the dimensions required by its prompt.

## Prompt-specific pressure tests

### Multi-team alignment

- Which distinct teams had different constraints?
- What decision or behavior did each change?
- What did the candidate do beyond scheduling coordination?

### Conflict and trust

- What was the other side right about?
- What did the candidate change in response?
- How was the relationship or subsequent collaboration different?
- Was repair demonstrated through sustained actions, not merely claimed?

### Technical investigation

- What symptom was observed?
- Which evidence ruled hypotheses in or out?
- What was the root cause?
- What exact mechanism changed?
- What regression or correctness risk did the fix introduce?
- How was it validated?

### Architecture

- What invariant or boundary was load-bearing?
- Which alternatives were credible?
- What future change became cheaper, and which risk was contained?
- What was designed versus actually implemented and shipped?

### Failure and feedback

- What did the candidate personally get wrong?
- What harm did it cause before recovery?
- How did they acknowledge and repair it?
- What later evidence proves behavior changed?

### Innovation

- What was new: mechanism, reframing, workflow, or combination?
- Which prior alternative or assumption failed?
- How did the idea change through iteration?

### Developing people

- Did the intervention build independent judgment rather than dependency?
- What scope, behavior, or capability changed?
- Is the claim individual mentoring or organizational capability?

### AI impact

- What did AI do, and what remained a human decision?
- What incorrect output or limitation was caught?
- How was correctness, privacy, or security verified?
- What advantage and consequence are attributable to the AI-enabled method?
- Was the learning reused or scaled?

## Causality ladder

Do not blur:

1. activity completed;
2. output shipped;
3. leading indicator moved;
4. causal user or business outcome measured;
5. durable organizational change observed.

A lower rung can still be valuable. Label it accurately.

## Fairness rule

Demand evidence appropriate to the claim. Do not require a user metric for a pure architecture decision, a ceremonial apology for trust repair demonstrated through sustained action, or organization-wide adoption for an individual mentoring prompt.
